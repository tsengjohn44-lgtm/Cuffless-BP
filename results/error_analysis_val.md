## How much of the error is a fixed offset per record

| | SBP | DBP |
|---|---:|---:|
| Share of the squared error that is each record's constant offset | 72% | 75% |
| Share of the squared error in the 10% of records with the largest offset | 44% | 57% |

A high offset share means the model is off by about the same amount for every window of a record: it misjudges that patient's baseline, rather than being noisy from window to window. Records have no patient IDs, so a record stands in for a patient.

## What-if: one calibration reading per record

Cuffless devices are often calibrated once against a cuff. To estimate what that would do, the first window of each record plays the calibration reading: its error is subtracted from every later prediction of that record, and only the later windows are scored. This is a different use case (it needs a reference reading per patient), not the headline result.
